NISHAN/ABOUT

Designed with structure. Finished with feeling.

I am Nishan, the designer behind Glitch Artworks. My work moves between branding, UI/UX, art, murals, 3D, and architecture - but the core stays the same: clear systems, strong visual tension, and work that feels intentional.

Founder of

Glitch Artworks is the studio practice where architectural structure meets visual instinct, built across digital, spatial, and hand-led work.

SEC-01 / Origin

Architecture brain, artist instinct.

I trained in architecture, so I naturally think in grids, proportion, rhythm, and how a person moves through a space. Glitch Artworks grew from that foundation into a wider visual practice - one where brand systems, interfaces, murals, objects, and digital experiments can all speak the same language.

Structure / Logic / System

Built on
blueprints.

I care about hierarchy, spacing, flow, and the invisible decisions that make a piece feel resolved. Even expressive work needs a skeleton.

Expression / Instinct / Soul

Driven by
contrast.

The work still needs tension: color, gesture, mood, and a point of view. That is where the system stops being cold and starts becoming memorable.
